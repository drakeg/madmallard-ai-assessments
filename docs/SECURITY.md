# Security and ethical operation

ActivityWatch: written employee consent, minimal aggregated data, no screenshots/keylogging; redact titles/URLs and define retention.

Elmo: clearly distinguish model answers from objective market share; document model, prompt, date, locale and sample size.

Browser Use: written authorization, allowlisted domains, sandbox/test accounts, synthetic identities, throttling, no payment or booking side effects, and deletion of test records. Do not submit forms to unconsenting businesses.

Outbound sales: no automated unsolicited sending; comply with applicable email and privacy laws. Require review and opt-out handling.

Sprint 0 dashboard is unauthenticated: expose only to trusted LAN/VPN for development. Do not deploy publicly until Sprint 1 auth and access controls are complete.
